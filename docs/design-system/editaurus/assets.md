The illustration has Polish alt text and depicts a fictional metaphor. The [OpenAI Europe Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/) assign rights in generated output to the user as between the user and OpenAI, subject to applicable law. No third-party print file was embedded.
- Fraunces regular and italic, Manrope in two weights, and Caveat come from the landing's Google Fonts distribution. The matching SIL OFL texts sit beside the local TTFs in `src/app/fonts/`. Home hero/role compositions use the landing families; editorial listing also uses Fraunces accents. Keep these contextual roles distinct from the DM Sans interface contract.
- The two inline annotation paths and hero layout rules are from the user's landing. The sticker footer and other landing assets are outside this slice.

The same generated-output provenance and [OpenAI Europe Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/) recorded for the hero apply to these variants.
